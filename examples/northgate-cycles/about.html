About Us

Northgate Cycles started in a garage on Mill Lane. Today we are a team of four mechanics who love bikes.

We believe every bike deserves good care, whether it is a racing bike or a shopping bike. We explain what we do, we only fix what needs fixing, and we never replace a part without asking you first.
