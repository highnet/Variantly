# Oak & Iron: Neon night market

A rain-slick night street of lit stalls: warm and electric tube light on near-black (deliberately not a synthwave sunset).

- **Colour:** night #0b0a0e, stall #1a171f; text #f2ece4, mute #bdb4c4; neon amber #ffb547, pink #ff4f8b, cyan #4cf2ff.
- **Type:** Tilt Neon for sign lettering (logo, headline, section heads, product names) with a layered text-shadow glow; Sora for body and controls.
- **Components:** each product is a stall with a striped, scalloped awning in its own neon colour and a glowing tube drawing; pill buttons outlined in pink tube light that fill on hover; a blurred reflection band under the hero reads as wet street.
- **Motion:** one authored moment, an occasional flicker on the pink headline line; off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Phone:** awning stripes cap each product band; glowing headline and cyan section signs.
