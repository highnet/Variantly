# Design: Memphis revival

Memphis as current brand studios use it: flat clashing blocks, a terrazzo-confetti ground, a squiggle or two, and fat geometric type - tidy, rounded and commercial rather than 1985 pastiche.

## Colour
- Paper `#fbf6ee` with terrazzo chips
- Cobalt `#2340ff`, tomato `#ff4d2e`, butter `#ffd84d`, lilac `#cdbbff`, mint `#a6e8c9`, pink `#ffb3cf`, ink `#141414`
- Colour blocks always carry ink text; cobalt carries white

## Type
- Unbounded 600/800 for logo, headings and prices
- Figtree 400-700 body
- Highlighter bar in pink under the italic-less 'made by hand.'

## Layout and components
- Hero composition of flat shapes: cobalt circle, butter half-disc, tomato triangle, dot grid, mint squiggle
- Section headings underlined with a tomato wave
- Feature tiles in lilac/mint/butter with one asymmetric corner each, middle one offset
- Product cards: 3px ink outline, coloured art panels with one big rounded corner
- Newsletter is a cobalt slab with a butter sun
- Motion: half-disc and squiggle rock gently; buttons tilt on hover; off under reduced motion

## Rules
- Copy, data, links and the newsletter form match the other variants; presentation only.
- Focus is always visible; body text >= 4.5:1; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
- Phone edition (phone.html/phone.css) skins the shared bands in ../phone.css and never resizes them.
