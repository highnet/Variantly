# Oak & Iron: Neo-brutalist web

Contemporary neo-brutalist web (Gumroad / Figma-community energy): thick black outlines, hard offset shadows, flat saturated fills.

- **Colour:** paper #fff6e5; ink #000; fills pink #ff90e8, yellow #ffc900, mint #3ddc97, blue #7aa7ff, orange #ff7a3d. Text is always black on fills (all pass AA).
- **Type:** Archivo Black uppercase for display; Space Grotesk for body, labels, prices and buttons.
- **Components:** 3px black borders throughout; 6px 6px 0 hard shadows on blocks, 4px on buttons; buttons lift on hover and press into their shadow on :active; the headline phrase sits on a tilted mint slab; a yellow starburst sticker; section titles are yellow label tabs; product cards have flat colour image wells.
- **Focus:** black outline plus a blue halo ring.
- **Phone:** same borders, shadows and fills; numbered yellow squares mark the sections.
