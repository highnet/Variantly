# Oak & Iron · Neo-folk craft (Dalarna kurbits)

## World
A contemporary furniture shop that borrows one specific tradition: **kurbits**, the decorative painting of Dalarna, Sweden (18th–19th c.), where rural painters decorated farmhouse walls, cupboards and chests with large gourd-shaped blooms rising from scrolling leaves. The tradition is credited on the page (footer) and here; we interpret its forms rather than copy any historic painting. Kurbits lived on furniture, which is why it suits a joinery and smithy.

## Tokens
- Ivory paper `#fbf7ef` / card `#f6efe3`; oxblood `#6b1e23` (headings, buttons); muted teal `#2c6461` (leaves, accents); burnt sienna `#b0502a` and ochre `#d6a043` only in ornament.
- Type: Young Serif (display) + Figtree (body). Dark theme via `html[data-theme="dark"]`.

## Components
- Hero "door panel": arched oxblood panel with ochre inlay line and grain noise, holding the kurbits bloom (inline SVG symbol).
- Sprig mark (logo, features); product cards on pigment-tinted grounds with a small painted brushstroke on each drawing.
- Pill buttons and inputs; double oxblood rule as section divider.

## Motion
One moment: the bloom paints itself in (leaves stroke-draw, then gourd layers swell). Off under reduced motion.
