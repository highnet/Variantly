# Oak & Iron: Contemporary spec sheet

A modern tech-diagram / product spec sheet on cyanotype blue. The page is one drawing sheet laid on a measured grid.

- **Colour:** cyan #0f3d75 sheet on a #0a2c57 grid ground; linework #eaf2ff; secondary text #b9cfee; highlight #ffe8a3 for focus and selection only.
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed 600 uppercase for display; IBM Plex Sans for body; IBM Plex Mono only for measurements, drawing numbers, labels, prices and the title block.
- **Components:** product plates are bordered drawings with dimension callouts (arrowheads, extension lines); a title block table carries project facts; note tags N1–N3 are circled callouts; buttons are flat white blocks that invert on hover.
- **Rules:** 1px solid lines for structure, dashed for guides. No shadows or gradients inside the sheet. Focus is a 2px dashed yellow outline.
- **Phone:** same grid, ink and mono labels; circled A/B/C callouts mark the sections.
