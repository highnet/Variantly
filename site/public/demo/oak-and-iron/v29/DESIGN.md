# Oak & Iron · Sensory 3D materials

## World
Soft, inflated, touchable forms rendered purely in CSS/SVG: a liquid-chrome blob, a translucent glacial-glass slab, an oxblood tufted cushion and sienna beads. Not Y2K chrome or liquid-glass UI: weighty, tactile objects in warm light.

## Tokens
- Warm ivory ground `#efeae4` with glacial-blue light pools; oxblood `#842a30` (inflated buttons), sienna `#b5582f`, muted teal `#3e6f73` labels, charcoal ink `#25272b`.
- Type: Unbounded (display) + Instrument Sans (body). Dark theme supported.

## Components
- Inflated buttons: radial gradient, inner highlight and shadow, soft drop; spring on hover, press-in on active.
- Frosted glass cards and nav capsule.
- Product renders as inflated tubes: one SVG symbol drawn three times (blurred shadow, coloured body, specular highlight).
- Material pebbles (sienna, chrome, glacier) as feature markers.

## Motion
One moment: the chrome blob slowly re-flows (border-radius morph). Off under reduced motion.
