# Oak & Iron: Acid graphics

A rave flyer laid out like an editorial page. A black ground carries liquid-chrome display type, an acid-green signal colour and a warped wireframe grid. It stays legible: chrome is used only for display text, and all body copy is solid.

## Colour
- Ground `#060606`, panel `#111311`, rules `#2c312b`
- Text `#ecefe9`, muted `#b3b9b0` (≥ 8:1 on ground)
- Acid `#c6ff1a`: the one signal colour, used for CTAs, prices and the line art. Text on acid is `#0a0a0a`.
- Chrome: a silver gradient clipped to display type (headline and section heads only)
- `html[data-theme="light"]` inverts to a pale ground with olive acid and gunmetal chrome

## Type
- Display: Unbounded 900, uppercase, tracking -0.03 to -0.04em
- Text: Instrument Sans 400/600
- Labels: Unbounded 700, 0.7rem, tracked 0.18em

## Shapes and components
- Pill buttons and inputs (999px radius); product plates with a 28px radius on a 24px wireframe grid
- Line drawings in acid with a soft green glow
- The warped SVG grid sits behind the hero only
- Hover: buttons lift and skew -6deg and turn silver; plate borders turn acid

## Motion
Transforms only, and all of it is off under reduced motion.
