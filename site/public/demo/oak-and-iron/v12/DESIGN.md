# Oak & Iron: Y2K chrome

Early-2000s web optimism (Frutiger Aero revival): brushed silver, liquid-metal display type, aqua gel buttons, holographic foil.

- **Colour:** silver #e6ebf1 to #cfd7e1 ground; ink #14243a; mute #3c4c63; aqua #00a6c8 / #006b86 / #7ff0ff; holographic conic foil (ice blue, lilac, butter, mint) as a blurred backdrop and on the feature tiles.
- **Type:** Michroma for display and labels (the headline has a chrome gradient fill with a thin ink stroke, as the brief pins); Exo 2 for body, prices and controls.
- **Components:** pill nav bar; glossy two-tone gel buttons with a top highlight and soft drop shadow; rounded 32px product pods with aqua bubble image wells; inset pill input.
- **Motion:** buttons lift 2px on hover with an expo ease-out.
- **Phone:** foil hero, chrome headline, aqua orb numerals, bubble product wells.
