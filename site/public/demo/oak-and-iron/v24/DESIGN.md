# DESIGN: Maximalist collage: cut, paste, sit down

Oak & Iron, Winter 2026 run, variant 4 of 10. Static HTML/CSS; fonts from Google Fonts; all art is inline SVG or CSS. phone.html/phone.css skin the shared phone bands in the same world.

## World
A scrapbook table: torn paper sheets with hard offset shadows, cut-out pictures (gingham, a sunset arch, a wood-grain panel, a teal circle with a stool), round stickers, masking tape and clashing patterns (stripes, polka dots, checkerboard). Every pattern sits behind a solid cream panel so text stays readable. Distinct from riso zine: no halftones or misregistration, full-colour paper cut-outs.

## Type
- Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headings and UI.
- DM Serif Display italic for accents ("made by hand", eyebrows, quote).

## Colour
Cream #f4ecdc, ink #1c1a1a, burnt sienna #c4552b, muted teal #1f6f6b, pink #f29bb4, mustard #e8b33a, cobalt #2a4bb8, oxblood #6e1423. Dark mode swaps the base and cards to warm charcoal.

## The digital effect
One holographic foil sticker (€500+) with a rotating conic gradient via @property (static under reduced motion).

## Accessibility
Body text meets 4.5:1; visible focus-visible outlines; nav, header, main and footer landmarks; no horizontal scroll at 390px; motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
