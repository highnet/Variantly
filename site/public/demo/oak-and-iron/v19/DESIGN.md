# Oak & Iron: Kinetic type

The typography is the layout. A single variable family (Bricolage Grotesque, with opsz, wdth and wght axes) is set at extreme scale contrast with tight tracking, plus one bold colour.

## Colour
- Paper `#f3f2ee`, ink `#0d0d0d`, muted `#45443f`
- Cobalt `#1d39f0`: the only colour, used for the lead word, the sign-off, CTAs and article numbers. Text on cobalt is white.
- Dark (`data-theme="dark"`): `#0c0c0e` ground, `#f1f0ec` ink, cobalt lifted to `#7d90ff`

## Type
- The hero is three scales: "Furniture" at up to 15.5rem (wdth 75, cobalt), "for real homes," at 300 weight, and "made by hand." right-aligned at 10.5rem
- Section heads and product names at wdth 75–80, weight 800, tracking -0.05em
- Body at 1.125rem, opsz 14

## Layout
- Full-bleed rows separated by 2px ink rules; products are typographic rows (name / drawing / buy)
- The footer ends on a giant cobalt "Oak & Iron" sign-off

## Motion
- Ready for motion but safe static: on load the lead word squeezes from wdth 100 to 75; on product hover the name widens and turns cobalt
- The static end state is the default, and reduced motion removes all animation
