# DESIGN: Cinematic drama: noir film stills

Oak & Iron, Winter 2026 run, variant 3 of 10. Static HTML/CSS; fonts from Google Fonts; all art is inline SVG or CSS. phone.html/phone.css skin the shared phone bands in the same world.

## World
Every section is framed like a still from an imaginary film: a letterboxed hero with velvet curtains, a hard key light, venetian-blind shadows and a stool with a long cast shadow. Product images are blacked-out frames lit by a single spot. Sections are slugged as scenes and shots; the footer ends with THE END.

## Type
- Bodoni Moda, often italic, for titles: high-contrast and theatrical.
- Courier Prime for body, slugs and UI: the screenplay voice.

## Colour
Near-black #0b0909, stage #140f0f, velvet #3b0b14, oxblood #7a1424 (buttons), ivory #efe4d0, spotlight gold #e9cc8f. Light theme is a cream script page with oxblood ink.

## The digital effect
A burned-in REC timecode in the hero slug, with a blinking dot; a faint film grain overlay and a subtle light flicker (all off under reduced motion).

## Accessibility
Body text meets 4.5:1; visible focus-visible outlines; nav, header, main and footer landmarks; no horizontal scroll at 390px; motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
