# DESIGN: Tactile imperfection: the hand-bound workshop ledger

Oak & Iron, Winter 2026 run, variant 1 of 10. Static HTML/CSS; fonts from Google Fonts; all art is inline SVG or CSS. phone.html/phone.css skin the shared phone bands in the same world.

## World
Raw ivory paper with an SVG fractal-noise grain, rust stitching (dashed rules and inset outlines), masking tape, ink stamps and a brushed ochre underline. Slightly rotated cards and uneven radii make every surface look cut and placed by hand.

## Type
- Fraunces (SOFT 100, WONK 1) for headings: soft, wobbly, warm.
- Newsreader for body copy.
- Kalam for handwritten marks: eyebrows, labels, links, the cart.

## Colour
Warm ivory #f3ead8 / #ebdfc6, ink black #1d1a16, rust #a23f1e (text #8c3417), muted ochre #b98a2c. Dark mode: walnut paper #1f1b16 with lightened rust and ochre.

## Components
Buttons are rust patches with a dashed stitch inset. Feature cards are stitched swatches with rubber stamps; product cards are taped polaroid-like sheets.

## The digital effect
One crisp aquamarine scanner line drifts over the oak sample board in the hero (static under reduced motion).

## Accessibility
Body text meets 4.5:1; visible focus-visible outlines; nav, header, main and footer landmarks; no horizontal scroll at 390px; motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
