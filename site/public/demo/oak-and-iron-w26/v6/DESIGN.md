# Puffed Letters

Letters are the furniture. A warm ivory sheet carries an inflated, balloon-soft wordmark, a handwritten arc of text, and product names set so large they become the product shots.

- **Type:** Bagel Fat One (inflated display, layered peach puff shadows), Caveat (handwriting for nav, notes, prices, accents), Fraunces with SOFT 100 / WONK 1 (headings and body).
- **Colour:** paper #f6ecdc, ink #2a1d18, oxblood #7b1f2b, burnt sienna #b4532a, muted teal #2c6766, puff #e7b08f. Dark theme via `html[data-theme=dark]`.
- **Components:** mixed-voice headings (`.puff` + `.hand`), warped SVG textPath tagline, prices circled in a hand-drawn ellipse, pillowy pill buttons with inset highlight, rounded field.
- **Motion (the one digital effect):** the wordmark breathes letter by letter, each glyph inflating and settling in sequence; the ampersand wobbles. Off under reduced motion.
- **Phone:** wordmark in the head band; each feature and product in a different lettering voice.
