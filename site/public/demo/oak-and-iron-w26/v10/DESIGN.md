# Oak & Iron · Quiet organic minimalism

## World
A calm, warm room: linen paper, clay, sage and mist pebble silhouettes, fine single-line drawings, wide margins. Warm, not clinical: every edge is soft and hand-shaped.

## Tokens
- Paper `#f8f4ee`, sand `#ebe1d3`, clay `#d9c3ae`, sage `#c9d0c6`, mist `#d7dfe0`, charcoal ink `#34302b`, burnt-sienna accent `#8a4a34` for links. Dark theme supported.
- Type: Instrument Serif (display, italic quote) + Hanken Grotesk (body).

## Components
- Hero cairn of three stacked organic stones with soft top light.
- Hairline-ruled feature list; products staggered on pebble-shaped plinths; outline pills for cart, solid charcoal pill for primary.

## Motion
One moment: a blurred pool of window light drifts slowly across the hero. Off under reduced motion.
